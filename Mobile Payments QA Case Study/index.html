Case study 1 · Banking & Fintech

Mobile wallet payments: France launch testing

Pre-launch and in-market testing of a mobile wallet in France: adding cards, paying in shops and on transit by contactless, and paying inside partner apps. Plus two finance-sector apps: a secure document-sharing app and a corporate banking portal.

Role
QA tester, manual (crowdtesting)
Period
2017 to 2018
Approach
Exploratory + scripted, field testing in real shops
Platforms
Android 6 to 8, smartwatch, iOS 10 to 11, Windows 10
Field test runs
133 recorded (117 passed)
Defects shown
12 (all approved by the client)

Confidentiality. This work was done under NDA. Client, product, merchant and bank names, accounts, card numbers, locations and internal IDs have been removed. Banks appear as Issuer A to D. Images are cropped and blurred. Requirements and test cases are rewritten in my own words from the test plans and my reports; they are not copies of client documents.

Project overview

Product, users and business context.

Product

A mobile wallet that stores debit and prepaid cards on an Android phone and pays by tapping the phone on a payment terminal, or by choosing the wallet at checkout inside partner merchant apps.

Users

Everyday shoppers and commuters in France, with cards from traditional banks and app-based banks, on a range of Android devices and mobile networks.

Business context

Launch of the wallet in a new market. Every failed payment at a till is seen by the customer, the cashier and the queue behind them, so reliability at launch decides whether people keep using the wallet.

Also in this study

A secure document-sharing app used by finance and legal teams (iOS), and a corporate banking portal for treasury clients (French localisation).

Scope

What was and wasn't tested.

In scope

  • Adding cards and issuer verification
  • Contactless payments in supermarkets, electronics and department stores, by phone and by smartwatch
  • Payments above the EUR 30 contactless limit
  • Rail and tram ticket machines
  • In-app payments in four partner apps: fashion retail, ride-hailing, two airlines
  • Tokenised and non-tokenised cards from four issuers
  • Access code and session handling (secure document app)
  • French localisation (corporate banking portal)

Out of scope

  • Server-side and API testing (no back-end access in crowdtesting)
  • Performance and load testing
  • Formal penetration testing
  • Card networks' and banks' own systems

Requirements and business rules

Functional requirements as I tested them. IDs are used in the traceability matrix.

IDRequirementBusiness rule
REQ-01Card provisioningA user can add an eligible card from any in-scope issuer and network (debit, prepaid; Visa, Mastercard) and see it ready to pay.
REQ-02Clear verification outcomeWhile the issuer verifies a card, the app shows progress; any failure tells the user what went wrong and what to do next.
REQ-03Contactless in-store paymentTapping the phone on an NFC payment terminal completes the payment at in-scope merchants.
REQ-04Consistent outcome, no false successThe phone and the terminal show the same result. The phone never confirms a payment the terminal refuses, and a failed attempt never creates a duplicate charge.
REQ-05Payments above the contactless limitAbove the card contactless limit (EUR 30 in France at the time), on-device verification (unlock or PIN) authorises the payment; the terminal must not refuse it afterwards.
REQ-06Transit acceptanceRail and tram ticket machines that accept contactless cards also accept the wallet; if a payment fails, the machine or phone shows a reason.
REQ-07Issuer parityAll in-scope issuers behave the same at the same terminal.
REQ-08In-app checkout shows eligible cardsAt a partner merchant's checkout, the wallet button appears and lists every eligible card, including non-tokenised cards where supported.
REQ-09In-app payment in the launch marketPayments through partner merchant apps are authorised for users in France.
REQ-10Declined payment recoveryAfter a decline, the user can retry or choose another card without losing the basket or booking.
REQ-11Access code reset needs re-authenticationChanging or resetting the app access code requires the current code or a verified identity.
REQ-12One credential prompt per sessionA server password prompt appears once; after the user enters or cancels it, it does not reappear on other pages or after relaunch.
REQ-13Complete French localisationWith the French locale selected, all headings, labels and help text are in French and no internal resource keys are visible.

Test strategy

Approach, environments and how evidence was collected.

Test types

  • Functional: provisioning, payment flows, error handling
  • Field testing: real purchases at real tills and ticket machines
  • Compatibility: device, OS, carrier and issuer combinations
  • Security: re-authentication and session handling
  • Localisation: French locale
  • Fix verification: re-tests on new builds

Approach and priorities

  • Test the highest-risk journeys first: paying at a till and adding a card
  • Change one variable at a time (issuer, device, terminal, tokenisation) to isolate the cause
  • Repeat each failure before reporting, and at more than one location
  • Compare with a working path (another issuer, another wallet) to route the bug to the right team

The client's test cases were short field procedures: install, add the card, make a purchase, record the result. I ran them 133 times and expanded them into the detailed cases below.

Environments

  • Samsung Galaxy S7 (Android 7.0, 8.0)
  • Huawei P8 Lite (Android 6), Huawei P9 Lite (Android 7)
  • Apple iPhone 6S (iOS 10.0.2, 11.0.1)
  • Windows 10, Chrome
  • Carriers: Bouygues Telecom, SFR, Free
  • Terminals: Ingenico iPP350, Verifone VX820, rail and tram ticket machines
  • Cards: 4 issuers, Visa and Mastercard, debit and prepaid

Evidence rules

  • Screen recording for every in-app bug
  • Phone video of the terminal for every in-store bug
  • Android bug report (device log) attached to functional bugs
  • App, services and OS versions; card type; amount; terminal model

Severity scale

SeverityMeaningExample
CriticalA core payment or onboarding journey fails with no workaround in the productD-01, phone shows success but the till refuses

Defect reports

Each report: environment, steps, expected and actual result, the exact error, business impact and how I isolated the cause.

D-01CriticalP1In-store NFC· Every time· 1 approved report

Phone shows payment success, but the terminal cancels the payment

Environment

Samsung Galaxy S7, Android 8.0 | Ingenico iPP350 terminal | Issuer A Mastercard debit | EUR 0.82, supermarket

Precondition

Card provisioned and set as default. Cashier confirmed the till accepts contactless payments.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Pick an item and go to the till.
  2. Unlock the phone and hold it near the terminal.
  3. Observe the phone: it vibrates and shows the success check mark.
  4. Observe the terminal: it shows PATIENTEZ (please wait).
  5. Repeat steps 2 to 4.

Expected

If the phone confirms success, the terminal approves the payment.

Actual

After a delay the terminal cancels the transaction and shows an error.

Error shown

Paiement sans contact refusé

Impact on users and business

This is the most damaging kind of payment failure: the customer believes they paid and may walk away, while the merchant has not been paid. It creates disputes at the till and erodes trust in a wallet being launched in a new market.

Investigation

Confirmed with the cashier that the terminal accepts contactless before testing, repeated the attempt, and checked the phone's NFC routing setting (see Evidence). Device log attached for developers.

Evidence

D-02CriticalP1In-store NFC· Every time· 1 approved report, reproduced at 3 merchants

Payments above EUR 30 are refused after successful on-device verification

Environment

Samsung Galaxy S7, Android 8.0, SFR | Verifone VX820 terminal | Issuer B Mastercard debit | EUR 31.66

Precondition

Card provisioned and set as default. Basket over the EUR 30 contactless limit.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Pick items worth EUR 30-35 and go to the till.
  2. Unlock the phone and tap the terminal.
  3. Complete the verification asked for and observe the phone.
  4. Observe the terminal.

Expected

Payment is approved. Above the contactless limit, on-device verification should replace the card PIN.

Actual

The phone shows a green check mark and the card logo, then the terminal shows PAIEMENT REFUSE.

Error shown

PAIEMENT REFUSE

Impact on users and business

Larger baskets fail at the till. Users who meet this once tend to return to the physical card, so the wallet loses its highest-value transactions. Reproduced at three merchants with different terminals, so it is not one faulty till.

Investigation

Reproduced at a food store, an electronics store and a department store. Recorded the full sequence on video. The device log at the moment of the attempt contains repeated errors while reading the wallet's card data (excerpt below); I flagged them for developer triage without claiming them as the root cause.

12:51:16.459 E Parcel : Class not found when unmarshalling: [wallet].firstparty.CardInfo
12:51:16.460 W Bundle : BadParcelableException: ClassNotFoundException when unmarshalling: [wallet].firstparty.CardInfo
12:51:17.082 E Parcel : Class not found when unmarshalling: [wallet].firstparty.CardInfo

D-03CriticalP1Transit· Every time· 1 approved report, rated very valuable

Rail ticket machines do not detect the phone at all

Environment

Samsung Galaxy S7 (Android 8.0), Huawei P8 Lite (Android 6), Huawei P9 Lite (Android 7) | Issuers A, B, C Mastercard debit | 4 stations | EUR 1.77

Precondition

Wallet provisioned on each device. Machine shows contactless acceptance.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Go to a rail ticket machine that accepts contactless.
  2. Select a destination and validate payment.
  3. Hold the phone on the reader, first unlocked, then locked.
  4. Observe the phone and the machine.

Expected

The payment completes. If it fails, a reason is shown on the machine or the phone.

Actual

Nothing happens on either device. The machine never detects the phone.

Error shown

None shown

Impact on users and business

Buying a train ticket is a daily, highly visible use case. A silent failure gives the user nothing to act on, and station staff cannot help.

Investigation

Isolated the variables: three devices from two manufacturers, three Android versions, three issuers, four stations, locked and unlocked. The failure was identical in every combination, which points to the machines or the acceptance setup rather than one device or card.

Evidence

Original screenshots show client branding and personal account details, so they are not published. Available on request in a redacted form.

D-04CriticalP1Transit· Every time· 1 approved report, rated very valuable

Tram ticket machine refuses Issuer B cards, while Issuer A works on the same machine

Environment

Huawei P8 Lite, Android 6, Free | Issuer B Mastercard debit | 2 tram stations | EUR 1.70

Precondition

Issuer B card set as default.

Steps to reproduce

  1. At a tram ticket machine, choose a 1-hour ticket.
  2. Pay by contactless with the phone.
  3. Repeat a few minutes later at a second station.
  4. Switch the default card to Issuer A and repeat at the same machine.

Expected

Payment completes with both cards.

Actual

Issuer B is refused every time. Issuer A succeeds at the same machine.

Error shown

Paiement sans contact refusé / Opération impossible

Impact on users and business

One bank's customers are excluded from transit payments. Without an issuer comparison this would look like a terminal fault and go to the wrong team.

Investigation

Comparing two issuers at the same terminal, minutes apart, removed the terminal and the device as causes and routed the bug to the issuer integration.

Evidence

D-05CriticalP1Provisioning· Every time· 1 approved report

Issuer D Visa debit card cannot be added: verification ends in a generic error

Environment

Samsung Galaxy S7, Android 8.0, SFR | Issuer D Visa debit

Precondition

Wallet installed and signed in with the test account.

Steps to reproduce

  1. Open the wallet and choose Add card.
  2. Enter all card details and accept the issuer terms.
  3. Wait for verification.

Expected

Card is added successfully.

Actual

After Contacting your bank and Your bank is verifying your card, an error appears and the card is not added.

Error shown

Double-check your card details. Something's not right. Please double-check the details and try again.

Impact on users and business

Onboarding blocker: without a card there is no payment. The message tells the user to re-check details that are correct, which sends them in circles and drives support contacts.
