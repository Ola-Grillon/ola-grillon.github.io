QA & Testing

I am a Manual QA Tester with several years of hands-on experience testing web applications, mobile applications, Smart TV applications and connected digital products. My experience spans multiple industries, including e-commerce, fintech, banking, healthcare, travel, transportation, betting, SaaS and entertainment.

I started my QA journey through uTest (now part of Applause), where I participated in software testing projects for different clients, products and testing environments. Over the years, I have developed practical experience in exploratory testing, functional testing, compatibility testing, regression testing, usability testing and defect reporting.

I enjoy investigating how applications behave in real-world conditions, identifying issues that affect users, understanding how problems can be reproduced and communicating findings clearly so that development teams can investigate and resolve them.

My approach to quality assurance goes beyond checking whether a feature works under normal conditions. I also consider unusual user behaviour, different devices, network interruptions, permissions, compatibility issues and the impact of defects on the overall user experience.

I hold the ISTQB Certified Tester Foundation Level (CTFL) certification and continue to develop my technical knowledge and testing skills.
